Courses & Mentorship

Teaching

Courses designed and taught at Harvard College and Harvard Extension & Summer Schools, and at Columbia and NYU (United States), the University of Fribourg (Switzerland), and Universidade Norte do Paraná (Brazil). Most Harvard Extension School courses are taught fully online.

The Courses

Reward, psychopathology, music, trauma, and scholarly craft — designed so students build real things.

Too Much of a Good Thing? The Psychology of Rewards

How reward shapes behavior, motivation, and psychopathology — and when a good thing turns. Harvard College & Extension School.

The Science and Psychology of Music

From auditory perception to music as medicine; students draft music-based policy proposals. Harvard College & Extension School; also taught at Columbia University.

Mechanisms & Markers of Psychopathology

Biological and behavioral markers of mental illness (formerly “Broken Brains”). Harvard College & Extension School; also taught at the University of Fribourg.

Intercultural Perspectives on Trauma

How culture shapes trauma expression and healing; students produce cross-cultural podcast episodes. Harvard College & Extension School; also taught at NYU as the graduate seminar “Cross-Cultural Studies on Traumatic Experiences.”

Introduction to Research in Psychopathology

Research design and methods for clinical science. Harvard Summer School.

Engaging the Scholarly Conversation

Graduate scholarly writing — entering, mapping, and contributing to an academic literature. Harvard Summer School.

Being Alive: The Science and Experience of Being Human

Being human as something to be studied — and something to be lived: empirical research paired, week by week, with first-hand experience.

In development · proposed Summer 2027

Earlier teaching abroad

Lecturer (Chargé de Cours) in the Department of Psychology, University of Fribourg, Switzerland (2018–2022) — courses included Applied Behavior Analysis and Psychopathologie des troubles de la personnalité — and Lecturer at Universidade Norte do Paraná, Brazil (2016–2017).

Recognized with multiple Harvard Certificates of Teaching Excellence and the 2026 Annamae & Allan R. Crite Prize. Courses partner with local NGOs so students build real mental-health resources for immigrant and refugee communities.
