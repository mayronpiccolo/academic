Intercultural Perspectives on Trauma

How culture shapes the expression of trauma — and what healing looks like across the world.

About the Course

Beyond WEIRD samples — trauma as culture expresses it.

Traumatic experiences vary in part due to the different adversities and experiences people from diverse cultures throughout the world face. And even though most of our understanding of psychological trauma is built on samples from Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, and Democratic (WEIRD) countries, many researchers generalize these findings to non-WEIRD populations.

This course looks at psychological trauma and post-traumatic stress through a multicultural lens, beyond the Western perspective. Can a global model of trauma fit all cultures? If not, how should we properly assess trauma using culturally valid methods? What is the impact of cultural experience on perceiving an event as traumatic? The course applies this foundation to a deeper understanding of cross-cultural trauma in veterans, refugees, and women who have experienced gender-based violence.
